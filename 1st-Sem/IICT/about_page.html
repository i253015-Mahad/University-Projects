About Us

Welcome to Mehnat, a platform built to connect hardworking individuals with the people and businesses that need them most. Founded by Mahad and Amad, Mehnat is all about making work simple, reliable, and fair — for everyone. Our goal is to bridge the gap between skilled workers and job providers, helping communities grow through trust and opportunity. Whether you need help with construction, repairs, deliveries, or specialized labor, Mehnat makes it easy to find the right person for the job — quickly and safely. At Mehnat, we believe in dignity in labor and efficiency in hiring. Every worker deserves recognition for their skills, and every employer deserves dependable service. That’s why we’re building a transparent, easy-to-use platform where both sides can thrive. Join us as we redefine the future of work — one job, one worker, one connection at a time. We have employed more than 100 workers in Pakistan and have made more than 1000 customers happy.

It is our mission to create ease for all.
